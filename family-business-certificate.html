Certificate Programme

Family Business & Wealth Management

For next-generation family business leaders balancing succession, investment, and innovation.

Programme Overview

For next-generation family business leaders balancing succession, investment, and innovation. You will learn how to structure family capital for new ventures, manage intergenerational wealth, evaluate external investment opportunities, and lead innovation inside an established family enterprise.

What You Will Learn

  • Family Business Governance & Succession Planning
  • Wealth Preservation & Growth Strategies
  • Impact Investing & ESG for Family Offices
  • Innovation Within Established Enterprises
  • Managing Family Dynamics in Business
  • Next-Gen Leadership Development

Key Facts

  • 6 months, part-time
  • Rolling admissions
  • $8,500 tuition
  • Certificate upon completion
