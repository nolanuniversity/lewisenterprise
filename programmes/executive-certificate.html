Executive Certificate

Funding Your Venture

Intensive 8-week programme for working entrepreneurs seeking angel or VC funding. Term sheets, valuation, and pitch preparation.

Programme Overview

Designed for working entrepreneurs who need capital now. This intensive 8-week programme covers: how to build an investor-ready financial model, what term sheets really mean, where to find angels and VCs, and how to negotiate better deals.

Evening and weekend classes. No degree required — just a venture and ambition.

8-Week Curriculum

Week 1: The Funding Landscape

Understanding funding sources: bootstrapping, angels, VCs, debt, grants, crowdfunding.

Week 2: Building Your Financial Model

Create a simple but powerful 3-statement model for your venture.

Week 3: Valuation for Early-Stage Ventures

Learn valuation methods: Scorecard, Berkus, VC Method, Comparable Analysis.

Week 4: The Perfect Pitch Deck

What investors actually want to see. Build your 10-slide deck.

Week 5: Term Sheets Demystified

Every clause explained: valuation, liquidation preferences, anti-dilution, board rights.

Week 6: Finding & Approaching Investors

Where to find angels and VCs. How to get warm introductions.

Week 7: Negotiation & Due Diligence

How to negotiate better terms. What investors will check.

Week 8: Live Pitch & Graduation

Present your venture to a panel of real investors.

Key Facts

  • 8 weeks, part-time
  • Hybrid format (online + in-person)
  • Multiple cohorts per year
  • $5,000 tuition
  • Certificate upon completion

Who Should Attend

  • Early-stage founders raising first round
  • Second-time entrepreneurs
  • Startup employees preparing to launch
  • Business owners seeking growth capital
