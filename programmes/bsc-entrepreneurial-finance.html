Undergraduate Degree

BSc Entrepreneurial Finance

Core finance curriculum with startup financial modeling, venture capital fundamentals, and a capstone venture pitch project.

Programme Overview

The BSc in Entrepreneurial Finance is designed for students who want to master both the science of finance and the art of venture creation. Unlike traditional finance degrees that focus on public companies, this programme teaches you how to fund, build, and scale new ventures.

You will learn to build financial models for companies that don't exist yet, negotiate term sheets, evaluate venture capital opportunities, and pitch to real investors. Graduates leave as founder-CFOs, venture analysts, or entrepreneurial finance specialists.

What You Will Learn

  • Startup Financial Modeling
  • Term Sheet Negotiation
  • Venture Valuation Methods
  • Investor Relations & Pitching
  • Venture Capital Fund Structures
  • Legal Aspects of Entrepreneurial Finance
