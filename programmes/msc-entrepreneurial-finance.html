Postgraduate Degree

MSc Entrepreneurial Finance

Advanced study of financing new and growing ventures, including angel investing, crowdfunding, venture debt, and exit strategies.

Programme Overview

At the intersection of corporate finance and venture creation lies entrepreneurial finance — a discipline with its own tools, models, and instincts. This MSc programme delivers advanced training in startup valuation, funding strategy, term sheet negotiation, and investor relations.

You will work on real venture cases, pitch to live investor panels, and graduate ready to lead finance for any early-stage company or join a venture capital firm.

What You Will Learn

  • Advanced Startup Financial Modeling
  • Valuation for Pre-Revenue Ventures
  • Term Sheet Anatomy & Negotiation
  • Venture Capital Fund Economics
  • Angel Investing & Crowdfunding
  • Exit Strategies & Liquidity Events
