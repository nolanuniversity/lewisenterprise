Undergraduate Degree

BSc Venture Capital & Private Equity

Focus on fund structures, deal sourcing, due diligence, valuation, and portfolio management for private markets.

Programme Overview

This is the first undergraduate degree designed specifically for students who want to become venture capitalists and private equity professionals. You will learn how funds are structured, how deals are sourced and evaluated, and how portfolio companies are managed for successful exits.

The programme combines rigorous financial training with hands-on deal experience. Students analyse real investment opportunities, conduct due diligence, and present investment memos to faculty and industry partners.

What You Will Learn

  • Fund Economics (2/20, Carried Interest)
  • Term Sheet Anatomy & Negotiation
  • Deal Sourcing & Due Diligence
  • Valuation Methods for Private Companies
  • Portfolio Management & Value Creation
  • Exit Strategies (IPO, M&A, Secondary)
