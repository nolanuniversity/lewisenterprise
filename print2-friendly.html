About the Lewis School

Most universities teach finance in one building and entrepreneurship in another. The Lewis School of Entrepreneurial Finance closes this gap. We are the first degree-granting institution dedicated entirely to the discipline of funding innovation.

Mission: To educate and empower a new generation of financial leaders who think like founders and founders who think like financiers.

Founded: 2025 | Named For: Mwaba Lewis, Founder of Nolan University

Academic Programmes

BSc Entrepreneurial Finance

Core finance curriculum with startup financial modeling, venture capital fundamentals, and a capstone venture pitch project. (3-4 years, full-time)

BSc Venture Capital & Private Equity

Focus on fund structures, deal sourcing, due diligence, valuation, and portfolio management. (3-4 years, full-time)

MSc Entrepreneurial Finance

Advanced study of financing new ventures, including angel investing, venture debt, and exit strategies. (1-2 years, full/part-time)

MSc Venture Capital & Growth Equity

Deep dive into institutional venture capital, growth equity, fund management, and LP-GP dynamics. (1-2 years, full/part-time)
