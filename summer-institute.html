Summer Programme

Summer Institute in Entrepreneurial Finance

Immersive 2-week programme for undergraduates from any discipline. No prior finance experience required.

Programme Overview

An immersive summer programme for undergraduates from any discipline — not just business or finance. You will learn the basics of startup funding, build a simple financial model, and pitch a venture idea to real investors. No prior finance experience required. Just curiosity and drive.

2-Week Curriculum

1
Welcome & Introduction
Why entrepreneurial finance matters. The funding lifecycle.
2
Financial Literacy Bootcamp
Basic accounting, reading financial statements.
3
Startup Financial Modeling
Build your first model for a simple venture.
4
Valuation for Beginners
How to estimate what a startup is worth.
5
Term Sheets 101
What investors actually put in contracts.
6-7
Weekend: Venture Pitch Workshop
Develop your venture idea with mentors.
8
Finding Investors
Where to find angels and VCs.
9
Pitch Practice
Refine your 5-minute pitch.
10
Final Pitch Competition
Present to real investors. Prizes awarded.

Key Facts

  • 2 weeks, full-time residential
  • July 2025
  • $3,000 (includes accommodation)
  • Certificate upon completion

Who Should Attend

  • Current undergraduates (any major)
  • Recent graduates exploring finance
  • Aspiring entrepreneurs
  • Students considering Lewis School degrees
