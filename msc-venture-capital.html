Postgraduate Degree

MSc Venture Capital & Growth Equity

Deep dive into institutional venture capital, growth equity, fund management, and LP-GP dynamics.

Programme Overview

This is the most comprehensive programme available for aspiring venture capital and growth equity professionals. You will learn fund economics (2/20, carried interest, management fees), LP-GP dynamics, valuation methods for high-growth companies, and the legal anatomy of term sheets.

Through case studies, guest lectures from practising VCs, and a capstone deal simulation, you will graduate ready to join a fund or start your own.

What You Will Learn

  • Fund Economics (2/20, Management Fees, Carried Interest)
  • LP-GP Relationship Management
  • Term Sheet Negotiation Masterclass
  • Deal Sourcing & Due Diligence
  • Portfolio Company Value Creation
  • Exit Strategies & Secondary Transactions
