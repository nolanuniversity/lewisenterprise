Scholarships & Financial Aid

Making entrepreneurial finance education accessible to talented students worldwide

Financial Support

Available Scholarships

The Lewis School offers a range of scholarships to support exceptional students

Founder's Scholarship

Full Tuition

Honouring the vision of Mwaba Lewis, this scholarship is awarded to students who demonstrate exceptional entrepreneurial potential and financial acumen.

  • Full tuition coverage
  • Mentorship from Mwaba Lewis
  • Guaranteed internship placement
Deadline: Priority - Dec 1, 2025

Mwaba Lewis Diversity Scholarship

Up to Full Tuition

Designed to increase representation in entrepreneurial finance, this scholarship supports students from underrepresented backgrounds.

  • Full or partial tuition
  • Access to diversity mentorship programme
  • Networking events with industry leaders
Deadline: Rolling

Merit Scholarship

Up to 50% Tuition

Recognising academic excellence and demonstrated achievement in finance or entrepreneurship.

  • 25% to 50% tuition reduction
  • Automatic consideration with application
  • Renewable annually based on academic performance
Deadline: Priority - Dec 1, 2025

Women in Venture Capital Scholarship

$25,000

Supporting women pursuing careers in venture capital and entrepreneurial finance.

  • $25,000 award
  • Mentorship from women VCs
  • Access to women in VC network
Deadline: Mar 1, 2025

Global Leaders Scholarship

$20,000

For international students who demonstrate leadership potential in their home countries.

  • $20,000 award
  • International student support services
  • Leadership development programme
Deadline: Rolling

Need-Based Grant

Variable

Providing financial support to students with demonstrated economic need.

  • Award amounts vary based on need
  • Requires FAFSA or equivalent
  • Renewable annually
Deadline: Rolling

How to Apply for Scholarships

Most scholarships are awarded through the standard admissions application. Simply indicate your interest in the application form. For need-based grants, please submit financial documentation.
